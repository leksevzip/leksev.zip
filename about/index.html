About leksev software labs

leksev software labs (officially leksev software labs IO) is a software development company founded in 2024. It was formed following a major corporate restructuring of Ksen Software, Inc. (its predecessor).
It's based in two countries in two different universes: Federation of United States (FUS) and Territory 42 (T42). And also, it's owned by a holding called Ksen, Inc.
The company specializes in making software mostly in C# (though other languages are used as well). It covers both B2B (Business to Business) and B2C (Business to Consumer) stuff.

About Half-Baked Games, Inc.

Half-Baked Games, Inc. (officially Half-Baked Games DO) is a division of leksev software labs founded in 2025 as a successor to Ksen Games.
It's based in Territory 42 and specializes in developing video games.
They used to make games in Unity (after Clickteam Fusion on Ksen Games era). But they switched to Godot and they're still using it.

About 1PO Networks

1PO Networks (1PO stands for One Person Online; officially 1PO Networks DO) is a division of leksev software labs founded in 2024 as frontend, backend and IoT division.
Just like HBG it's based in Territory 42 and specializes in making websites, APIs, VPNs and other web stuff.
